LLM internals







Three self-contained pages explaining how large language models work, from the matrix arithmetic up to why your API bill is mostly cache reads. They were written for someone comfortable with vectors, dot products and optimisation who has never had reason to learn machine-learning vocabulary — so every ML term is defined where it first appears, and no mechanism is introduced before the problem it solves.



Each page has a TL;DR at the top and a glossary at the bottom. They build on each other in order, but any one works alone.

What is real and what is illustrative




Worth being explicit, since the interactive parts could otherwise be taken for more than they are.



Computed live, and correct: the attention arithmetic (dot products, scaling, masking, softmax, weighted sum) on a hand-picked 4×4 toy; the sinusoidal positional encoding; the sampling softmax under temperature and top-p; every figure in the cache calculator and TTL simulator.



Illustrative, and labelled as such in place: the tokeniser is a heuristic, not a learned merge table; embedding-table values are seeded random numbers; the multi-head attention patterns are hand-drawn to show what specialised heads look like, not extracted from a trained model.



Prices and thresholds in the cache page were checked against Anthropic's documentation in September 2026 and are the sort of thing that moves — the page links its sources so you can re-check before acting on any number.
